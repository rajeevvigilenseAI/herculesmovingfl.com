How Much Does a Local Move Cost in South Florida?

Updated September 2026  |  Miami-Dade, Broward, Palm Beach

The honest answer is "it depends." That is not a dodge. A local move in Miami, Fort Lauderdale, or West Palm Beach is priced from a short list of real inputs: how many people are on the crew, how long the job takes, how far the truck drives, what is actually coming with you, and how hard the buildings are to work. Once you know those factors, you can estimate your own move in the right ballpark, then get a written number in a few minutes instead of guessing from a price list that was never going to match your house.

What actually determines your moving cost

Five things move the quote. None of them is a mystery, and none of them should show up as a surprise fee on move day.

Crew size

A two-person crew and a four-person crew do not cost the same, because they do not finish in the same amount of time. A studio with a freight elevator may only need two movers. A three-bedroom house with a garage full of tools usually needs more hands so the day does not stretch into overtime. The right crew is the one that matches the inventory, not the smallest crew that can theoretically finish if nobody takes a break.

Time

Time is the other half of crew size. Stairs, a slow passenger elevator, a long walk from the only legal parking spot, and a second-floor condo with no freight lift all add minutes that add up. So does the drive between origin and destination when traffic on I-95 or the Turnpike is stacked. A written estimate has to assume those conditions, not a perfect empty loading zone at 10 a.m. on a Tuesday.

Distance

A move across the same neighborhood is not the same job as a move from Kendall to Coral Springs or from Fort Lauderdale to Boca Raton. Cross-county trips inside South Florida are still local work for us. They are priced on the actual distance and time, not treated like an interstate shipment. If you are leaving one county for another, see how South Florida movers handle tri-county jobs with one crew and one quote.

Volume (what you are actually moving)

A one-bedroom with a mattress, a sofa, and fifteen boxes is not a four-bedroom with patio furniture and a garage. Volume matters. What does not matter here is a weight minimum or a size minimum. You do not pay for empty truck space you did not use, and you do not get billed as if a studio were a three-bedroom. That is the point of a quote built around your inventory instead of a published rate card.

Access

South Florida access is its own category. High-rise elevator reservations, Certificates of Insurance, a loading dock that only opens in a two-hour window, a gated community with a designated entrance, street parking that disappears by noon: each of those can add time if nobody planned for it. A Miami movers crew that already knows Brickell dock rules, or a Broward crew that has reserved Las Olas freight, finishes closer to the estimate than a crew discovering the building on the morning of the move.

What is included in a Hercules quote (so there are no surprises)

When we send a written estimate, the truck, fuel, blankets, wrap, and basic disassembly and reassembly are already in it. You should not be asked on the driveway whether you want pads on the dresser. That is how the job is supposed to be done.

The industry's bad habit is the opposite: a low number on the phone, then add-ons once the furniture is on the truck. Fuel. Blankets. A "long carry." A "stair fee" that was never mentioned. By then you are not shopping anymore. You are negotiating with a crew that already has your sofa. Our differentiator is boring on purpose. The quote is the quote. No hidden fees, and no rewriting the job after you have already taken the day off work.

If the move is a house, a townhome, or an apartment, start with residential moving services. Specialty pieces such as a piano are scoped separately so they are not smuggled into a generic hourly number.

Why we do not publish a fixed price list

A fixed list would be easy to rank for and easy to be wrong. Two "two-bedroom" moves in the same zip code can differ by a full crew-hour because one has a freight elevator and the other is a walk-up with a 200-foot carry from the only legal truck spot. Publishing a single number for "a local move" would either pad every job or bait people into a quote that cannot survive move day.

An honest price depends on your actual move. A written estimate up front beats a misleading number on a blog. Every Hercules quote is guaranteed in writing. If the scope does not change, the price does not change.

How to get an accurate estimate in minutes

You do not need a salesperson in the living room to get close. Have a sense of rooms and heavy pieces, the two addresses or at least the two cities, and any building rules you already know (COI, elevator window, gate hours). Put those into the free estimate on the homepage. If something unusual is in the mix (a safe, a piano, a third-floor walk-up), say so. The faster those details are in the quote, the less anyone has to "adjust" later.

South Florida moving cost FAQs

Do you charge by the hour or a flat rate?
Local South Florida moves are typically quoted from crew size and the time the job is expected to take, with access and distance folded into that written number. You get the estimate before move day, not a surprise after the truck is loaded.
Are there hidden fees or surprise charges?
No. A Hercules quote is written up front and includes the truck, fuel, blankets, wrap, and basic disassembly and reassembly. The price you are told is the price you pay.
Do you have a minimum weight or size?
No. There is no weight minimum and no size minimum. You pay for the move you actually have, whether it is a studio, a few rooms, or a full house.
Do cross-county moves cost more?
Cross-county jobs inside South Florida are priced honestly on distance and scope, not treated like an interstate shipment. The extra drive time is in the written estimate, with no surprise long-distance surcharge for a Miami-to-West-Palm-style trip. See our South Florida service area.
