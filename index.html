NO WEIGHT MINIMUMS

Some moving companies charge a minimum weight for each shipment, meaning small move customers pay for weight they don't have. We are here to fill a niche serving small move customers, meaning you won’t be charged a minimum weight.

NO SIZE MINIMUMS

Some moving companies charge for a minimum load size on all moves. This means that you are being charged for space in their truck that you are not using. That is just not fair. Call on us for your next small move and avoid being charged for space you don't need.

NO HIDDEN FEES

Nothing will ruin your move more than being sold on one price and being charged another. When we quote your move, we quote it accurately. All fees are included in your guaranteed quote so you will never suffer from sticker shock or hidden fees.

SAME GREAT SERVICE

Just because yours is not a big ticket move doesn't mean that you should expect less than stellar service. We treat all movers with the goal of earning future business. Whether you are moving a truckload or a single item, you will receive extraordinary service.
