Small Moves Are What We Do

Most moving companies are built around full houses and full trucks, so a small job gets a minimum charge, a long wait, or no call back. Hercules Local Movers was set up for the opposite: the couch that will not fit in your car, the studio across town, the few pieces going to a new apartment or a storage unit. Small moves are not a side job for us - they are most of our week.

What Counts as a Small Move

  • Single items - a sofa, mattress, dresser, table, TV or appliance, including store and online-marketplace pickups.
  • A few items - several pieces of furniture and boxes going to the same place.
  • Studios and 1-bedroom apartments - including condos with freight elevators and loading docks.
  • Partial loads - part of a home going to a new place, a family member, or a second property.
  • Storage units - moving things into, out of, or between storage units.
  • Small office moves - a few desks, filing cabinets or office machines. See commercial moves.

Why No Minimums Matters

Many movers charge a minimum weight or a minimum load size on every job. On a small move that means paying for pounds you do not have and truck space you do not use. We do not do that. There is no weight minimum and no size minimum, and every fee is in the written estimate, so a two-piece move is priced like a two-piece move.

Small Move FAQs

How much do small movers cost in Miami and Fort Lauderdale?
Our online estimates for a local small move (under 50 miles) start at $299 for a 2-hour job, which covers most single-item moves, a few pieces of furniture, studios and 1-bedroom apartments. A 2-bedroom is usually estimated at $449 for 3 hours. A service charge applies to all moves and is confirmed in your written estimate, and the final bill follows the actual time.
Is there a minimum weight or truck size?
No. We do not charge weight minimums or size minimums, so you never pay for truck space or pounds you are not using.
Can you move just one item?
Yes. Single items are one of our most common jobs: a sofa, a mattress, a dresser, an appliance, or a piece you bought from a store or an online marketplace. Pianos and fine art are handled by our specialty crew.
Do you handle partial loads and storage units?
Yes. We move partial loads between homes, into and out of storage units, and to or from a family member’s place, priced on time rather than a full-truck rate.
Are you licensed and insured?
Yes. Hercules Local Movers is licensed and insured, FL Mover Registration #IM150012.
