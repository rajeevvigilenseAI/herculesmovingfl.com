One Local Crew Across Palm Beach County

Palm Beach County moves tend to be a different kind of job. More single-family homes, more established neighborhoods, and more gated communities than anywhere else in South Florida. Hercules Local Movers covers all of it - West Palm Beach and downtown, the waterfront, and south county through Boca Raton, Delray Beach, and Boynton Beach - with one local crew and the same flat, honest pricing whether it is a condo or a full house.

No weight minimums, no size minimums, no surprise fees. You get a written quote up front and the price you are told is the price you pay.

Gated Communities & HOA Move-In Rules - Handled

Palm Beach County has more gated and HOA-managed communities than almost anywhere in the state, and each one has its own move rules: gate check-in and vehicle registration, approved move-in days and hours, Certificate of Insurance (COI) requirements, and designated entrances or loading points. A crew that shows up without clearing these ahead of time gets stopped at the gate.

We handle the community requirements before move day - registering with the gate, providing the COI, and scheduling within the association's approved windows - so your move runs on time instead of stalling at security. From 55+ communities to guard-gated estates, it is routine for us.

Homes & Larger Moves

Most of our Palm Beach County work is single-family and larger home moves. Our crews come equipped for it: quilt-wrapped furniture, proper packing for fragile and high-value pieces, and basic disassembly and reassembly of beds and larger furniture included in the quote. Truck, fuel, blankets, and wrap are all part of the price - not add-ons sprung on you at the end.

Downtown, CityPlace & Condos

For downtown West Palm and CityPlace-area condos, we coordinate building requirements ahead of time - COI, elevator reservation, and loading-dock timing - so move day stays on schedule.
