Piano Movers

Moving a piano is no easy task and it is certainly not something you want to attempt on your own. Pianos are heavy, bulky and difficult to replace if damaged. Finding an experienced mover is a necessity when it comes to relocating this fragile and expensive instrument. Whether it is a spinet, upright, baby grand or concert grand, we can move your piano with the care and respect that it deserves.

Pool Table Movers

A pool table can weigh up to a half-ton or more, so it’s essential that you have the proper training and tools for relocating your pool table. Trying to move a table on your own or without the right equipment could lead to serious injury or a ruined table. When you hire professional movers to do the job, you can rely on our years of expertise and knowledge to safely disassemble, move, and re-install your billiard table.

Fine Art Movers

Our fine art moving process includes everything necessary to make the relocation of your fine art a success. We handle all of your fine art pieces with the utmost care. We utilize high-quality packing materials and museum-level relocation standards to ensure that your precious artworks arrive safely and unscathed. From masterpiece paintings to fine art sculptures, we can professionally move any fine art piece.

Antiquities Moving

Do not trust your prized antiques to amateurs.

High-rise Specialists

Moving into a high-rise building is a unique experience and presents many unique challenges. And while the views and amenities might be spectacular, the move-in process can be tricky if you aren’t prepared for it. Most high-rise buildings have very specific rules with regard to moving furnishings in and out. We can help guide you through this somewhat cumbersome process and make your high-rise move less stressful.
