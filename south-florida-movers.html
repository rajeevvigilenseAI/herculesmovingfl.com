One Crew Across Miami-Dade, Broward, and Palm Beach

Most local movers work one county and hand anything bigger to a long-distance carrier. We don't. Hercules Local Movers covers all three South Florida counties - Miami-Dade, Broward, and Palm Beach - with the same crew, the same flat honest pricing, and the same care whether you're moving five miles or across the metro.

That means if you're leaving a condo in Miami for a place in Boca Raton, or an apartment in Fort Lauderdale for a house in West Palm Beach, you're not getting passed off or hit with interstate-style pricing. It's one local move, handled start to finish by people who know the region.

Moving Across County Lines? We Do That Every Week.

Miami to West Palm. Kendall to Coral Springs. Hollywood to Delray. Cross-county moves are routine for us, and we price them honestly on distance - no weight minimums, no size minimums, no surprise "long distance" surcharge for a move that's still within South Florida.

You get a written quote up front that reflects the actual distance and scope, and everything's included: truck, fuel, blankets, wrap, and basic disassembly and reassembly. The price you're quoted is the price you pay.

High-Rises and Certificates of Insurance

From the Brickell and downtown Miami towers to Las Olas in Fort Lauderdale to the West Palm waterfront, South Florida high-rises and managed communities almost always require a Certificate of Insurance (COI) before move day. We handle COIs as routine across all three counties - give us your building and we coordinate the certificate with property management ahead of time.
