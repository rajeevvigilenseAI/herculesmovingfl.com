Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 18, 2026

Hercules Local Movers (“Hercules Movers,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) operates herculesmovingfl.com and related moving services in Florida. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

By using our website, requesting an estimate, or confirming a reservation, you agree to this Privacy Policy and our Terms and Conditions.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect:

  • Contact details you provide, such as name, email address, and phone number.
  • Move details, such as origin and destination locations (city, ZIP, or full address), home size, preferred move date, arrival window, and notes you choose to share.
  • Communications you send us by form, email, phone, text/SMS, or social media.
  • Technical data such as IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited, and approximate location derived from IP, collected through cookies and similar technologies.
  • Advertising and conversion data related to ads you interact with (for example, Google Ads), including whether a reservation or other valued action was completed.

2. How We Use Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide estimates, reserve moves, and deliver moving services.
  • Contact you by phone, text/SMS, or email about your inquiry, estimate, reservation, scheduling, payment, claims, or service updates.
  • Operate, secure, and improve our website and booking tools.
  • Measure advertising performance and understand which ads lead to inquiries or reservations.
  • Comply with law, prevent fraud or abuse, and protect our customers, crew, and business.

3. Calls, Texts/SMS, and Email

When you submit an estimate request or confirm a reservation and provide a phone number or email, you consent to receive transactional communications from Hercules Movers related to your move. That may include calls, text/SMS messages, and emails about scheduling, confirmations, updates, payment, delivery, and claims.

Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies based on your move. You can opt out of promotional or non-essential texts by replying STOP. For help, reply HELP or call 1 (754) 354-2008. Consent to receive texts or calls is not a condition of purchasing moving services.

We do not sell your phone number. Text/SMS consent is separate from other consents and is not shared with third parties for their own marketing.

4. Cookies, Analytics, and Advertising

Our site uses cookies and similar technologies, including Google tags, to:

  • Keep the site working (for example, form and booking session needs).
  • Understand site traffic and performance.
  • Measure Google Ads conversions (such as a completed reservation) and improve ad relevance.

Google may collect information about your visits to this and other sites. You can learn more in Google’s Privacy Policy and manage ad settings at adssettings.google.com. Most browsers let you block or delete cookies; doing so may affect some site features.

5. How We Share Information

We share information only as needed to run the business, including with:

  • Service providers that help us host the site, process forms, send email or SMS, store booking data, or run advertising and analytics (for example, Google, and form-delivery services used for estimate or reservation emails).
  • Professional advisors such as insurers, attorneys, or accountants when reasonably necessary.
  • Authorities when required by law or to protect rights, safety, and property.

We do not sell your personal information for money. We do not share mobile numbers with third parties for their independent marketing.

6. Data Retention

We keep personal information only as long as reasonably needed for the purposes above, including move records, customer service, legal, tax, insurance, and dispute-resolution needs, unless a longer period is required by law.

7. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards appropriate for a small moving business website. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your Choices and Rights

You may:

  • Request access to or correction of personal information we hold about you.
  • Ask us to update or delete contact details when we are not required to keep them.
  • Opt out of marketing emails by using the unsubscribe link or contacting us.
  • Opt out of texts by replying STOP.
  • Control cookies through your browser and Google ad settings.

Depending on where you live, additional privacy rights may apply. Contact us using the details below and we will respond as required by applicable law.

9. Children

Our website and services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

10. Third-Party Links

Our site may link to third-party sites. Their privacy practices are their own. Review those policies before providing information to them.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The effective date above will change when we do. Continued use of the site after an update means you accept the revised policy.
