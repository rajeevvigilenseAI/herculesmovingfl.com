LICENSED FLORIDA MOVERS

Florida state law requires that licensed movers be insured, have licensed vehicles that meet certain safety and other requirements, and provide for a system in which complaints can be made and dealt with properly. Even if you do not hire us, be sure to protect yourself by hiring a mover licensed in the state of Florida.

Cleaning and Staging

Packing Services

Storage Options

High-rise Specialists

We can help guide you through this somewhat cumbersome process and make your high-rise move less stressful.
