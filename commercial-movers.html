Flexible Scheduling

Business interruption is a major concern for our commercial clients and relocating your business operations will undoubtedly result in some downtime. We offer flexible scheduling options to help mitigate downtime and get your business up and running as quickly as possible. Need to make your move overnight? On the weekend? We can help with flexible scheduling options to meet any timeline.

Office Equipment Specialists

Your office equipment is an essential part of your business operations and represents a significant financial investment. Damage or breakage during a move is simply unacceptable. Our office equipment specialists have the experience and know-how to safely pack and transport everything from computer equipment and flat screens to copy machines and printers. We'll make certain that your equipment arrives safely.

Packing Services

Packing your business assets is one of the most time consuming aspects of a commercial move. Our professional packing services include organized file transfer, electronic wire labeling, and even workspace-specific labeling, so that your staff is not hunting for their purple stapler two days after the move. We can pack and unpack your entire office to help ensure that your business does not miss a beat.

Pre-Move Consultations

We understand that as a business owner or manager you want your business move to be as organized and efficient as possible. Our pre-move consultations allow us to understand your priorities, specialty items, specific requirements, and time constraints. We want to make your move as seamless as possible and a thorough understanding of your unique needs helps us do just that.

High-rise Specialists

If your business operations are moving to or from a high-rise building there are many additional logistics that need to be considered. Many high-rise commercial buildings limit moves to specific days and times, require parking permits, use of freight elevators, and have other rules and regulations with regard to relocations. Our experienced movers can help you navigate this often complex process.

Freight Delivery

Do you have freight that needs to be delivered? We can handle all of your freight delivery needs. From large and cumbersome items to fragile and irreplacable artwork and antiques. Our professional movers will securely pack and pad your items, transport them in our air ride trucks and vans, and deliver them with care directly to your door. Get your freight delivered with the care and professionalism it deserves.
